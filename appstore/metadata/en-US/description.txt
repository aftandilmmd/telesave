Drop a file on your menu bar and it lands in Telegram.

TeleSave uploads anything you drag onto it to your Telegram Saved Messages — the cloud drive you already have on every device — or to any chat, group or channel you choose.

DRAG, DROP, DONE
• Drag files toward the menu bar and drop them on the shelf under the TeleSave icon.
• Or paste with ⌘V, choose files, or right-click in Finder › Send to TeleSave.
• Folders go as one zip or file by file.

ORIGINAL QUALITY
• Photos and videos are sent as documents, in full resolution, with their file names.
• Prefer compressed media or albums? One switch in Settings.

SEND IT ANYWHERE
• Saved Messages by default, or a person, group, channel or forum topic.
• TeleSave checks you may post there, and highlights any target other than Saved Messages so nothing goes to the wrong chat.

RELIABLE
• Several uploads at once, with progress right in the menu bar.
• Uploads pause when you go offline and resume when you are back — even after a restart.

YOUR FILES AT A GLANCE
• Browse, search, preview, download and delete the files of a chat.
• Search ignores case and accents.

PRIVATE
• Files go straight from your Mac to Telegram. TeleSave has no server and collects no data.
• Sign in with a QR code or your phone number; your password never reaches TeleSave.

Available in English, Turkish, Azerbaijani and Russian. Files up to 2 GB (4 GB with Telegram Premium).

TeleSave is an independent app and is not affiliated with Telegram.
