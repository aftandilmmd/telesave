TeleSave Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

TeleSave uploads files you choose to your own Telegram account. It is built so that nobody but you and Telegram ever handles your data.

What TeleSave collects

Nothing. TeleSave has no server of its own, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The developer does not receive any information about you, your account or your files.

Your files and your Telegram account

Files you drop, paste or choose are sent directly from your device to Telegram, to the chat you selected (Saved Messages by default). You sign in with Telegram's own login (QR code or phone number); TeleSave never sees your Telegram password. Your Telegram session is stored encrypted on your device, and signing out removes it.

How Telegram handles your data is described in the Telegram Privacy Policy.

Network connections

  • Telegram — to sign in and to upload, list, download and delete your files.
  • Configuration file — TeleSave may download a small signed configuration file from GitHub (for example, to show an announcement). Only a standard download request is made; nothing about you is sent.

Files on your device

TeleSave only reads files you give it. It writes only to its own storage and, when you ask it to download a file, to your Downloads folder.

Children

TeleSave does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Contact
